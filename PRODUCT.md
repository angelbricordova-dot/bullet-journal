# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) deployed on Vercel, chosen because the owner will host it on Vercel. Data lives in a private Postgres database (Neon, connected through the Vercel Marketplace) behind a single-owner password. Desktop shows a CSS 3D ring-bound notebook; phones get a single-page view of the same notebook.

## Users

One user: Alejandro, 21, in Maracay (Aragua, Venezuela). Christian, freelance web/app developer, seller and video editor. Uses it on both phone and laptop, equally. It is a private tool, not a product for others; nobody else signs in.

He is at a turning point: wants to break bad habits (late sleep and waking, reels first thing in the morning for 2–3 hours, untidy room and clothes, inconsistent tooth brushing, skipping the gym he already paid for, lust), get closer to God, rebuild income after losing two clients, and learn to manage money (money itself is tracked in his phone app Real, not here).

## Product Purpose

A 3D virtual bullet journal where he documents his life day by day and sees his progress over time. The core is personal challenges and self-improvement: daily habits, streaks on things he is quitting, honest logging of falls with reflection, faith practice, and money discipline. Success means he opens it every day, the streaks grow, and the year view shows a life changing.

## Positioning

Not a generic habit tracker: it is his own notebook. Physical-journal interactivity (open the cover, turn pages, place stickers, write on any day) plus ink, stickers, tape and photos on any page, shaped around faith, purity and discipline rather than productivity scores.

## Operating Context

- Daily ritual: morning check-in (first hour without phone), night check-in (habits, sleep time, gratitude).
- Moments of temptation or doom-scrolling: needs a fast "momento difícil" escape with his own reasons, a verse and a concrete action.
- Important dates: Angélica's birthday (22 Nov 2026) and pay dates.
- Money lives in his app "Real". He exports it weekly or monthly; the notebook imports that export (CSV/JSON) into the month's summary.
- Day one of the change: 2026-11-11.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Today page: rapid log (task, event, note; done, migrated, cancelled), habit checklist, sleep/wake time, mood, gratitude, notes, stickers.
- Challenges: abstinence streaks (reset with a reflection, no shaming) and habit-linked streaks; "momento difícil" button always reachable.
- Progress: month trackers and the monthly summary.
- Faith: verse of the day (Reina-Valera 1960), prayer requests (pending/answered), gratitude.
- Month: welcome page with goals and a calendar he writes in; trackers (habit dot grid, sleep chart, mood); monthly summary with the Real import.
- Books list and a free page.
- Ink, stickers, washi tape and taped photos available on every page and the cover (not a separate canvas section).
- The cover always shows the current month and the days left.
- Customization: cover name and color, paper style, habits list, reasons, dates.
- Privacy: everything is behind his password; data must never be exposed publicly. Backup export/import.
- Spanish (Venezuela) UI copy, "tú".

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual constraints from the owner: 3D and interactive like the reference planner (ring binder, cover that opens, pages turned by dragging, tabs, sticker sheets and washi tape on the desk, rotate and zoom); structure taken from his own paper bullet journal (index, month welcome + goals, habit and sleep trackers, books, free pages with taped photos); masculine and serious; leather-notebook direction (dark leather cover, cream paper, black ink, brass details, red ribbon). Interactive like a physical bullet journal (reference he liked: an interactive 3D planner with cover, page turns, tabs, stickers).

## Evidence on Hand

Only the owner's own data. No testimonials, no other users, nothing to fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Honest, not punishing: falls are logged with reflection and the counter restarts; victories are counted too.
2. Daily first: the today page must load fast and be completable in under two minutes.
3. Faith is woven in, not bolted on: verses and prayer sit next to the habits they support.
4. Private by default: nothing renders without the password.
5. His notebook: personalizable, tactile, worth opening.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Must work one-handed on a phone and with keyboard on laptop; respect reduced-motion.
