# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) deployed on Vercel, chosen because the owner will host it on Vercel. Data lives in a private Postgres database (Neon, connected through the Vercel Marketplace) behind a single-owner password. Free-form canvas uses Excalidraw (MIT).

## Users

One user: Alejandro, 21, in Maracay (Aragua, Venezuela). Christian, freelance web/app developer, seller and video editor. Uses it on both phone and laptop, equally. It is a private tool, not a product for others; nobody else signs in.

He is at a turning point: wants to break bad habits (late sleep and waking, reels first thing in the morning for 2–3 hours, untidy room and clothes, inconsistent tooth brushing, skipping the gym he already paid for, lust), get closer to God, rebuild income after losing two clients, and learn to manage money.

## Product Purpose

A virtual bullet journal where he documents his life day by day and sees his progress over time. The core is personal challenges and self-improvement: daily habits, streaks on things he is quitting, honest logging of falls with reflection, faith practice, and money discipline. Success means he opens it every day, the streaks grow, and the year view shows a life changing.

## Positioning

Not a generic habit tracker: it is his own notebook. Physical-journal interactivity (open the cover, turn pages, place stickers, write on any day) plus a free canvas for drawing and writing, shaped around faith, purity and discipline rather than productivity scores.

## Operating Context

- Daily ritual: morning check-in (first hour without phone), night check-in (habits, sleep time, gratitude).
- Moments of temptation or doom-scrolling: needs a fast "momento difícil" escape with his own reasons, a verse and a concrete action.
- Important dates: Angélica's birthday (22 Nov 2026) and pay dates.
- Money context: USD, low balance (180 USD on 2026-11-11), two current clients under 500 USD/month combined, next income roughly a month away.
- Day one of the change: 2026-11-11.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Today page: rapid log (task, event, note; done, migrated, cancelled), habit checklist, sleep/wake time, mood, gratitude, notes, stickers.
- Challenges: abstinence streaks (reset with a reflection, no shaming) and habit-linked streaks; "momento difícil" button always reachable.
- Progress: year in pixels, per-habit heatmaps, streak history.
- Faith: verse of the day (Reina-Valera 1960), prayer requests (pending/answered), gratitude.
- Money: balance, income/expense log, clients, savings goal.
- Canvas: multiple free-form boards (draw, write, shapes, images).
- Customization: cover name and color, paper style, habits list, reasons, dates.
- Privacy: everything is behind his password; data must never be exposed publicly. Backup export/import.
- Spanish (Venezuela) UI copy, "tú".

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual constraints from the owner: masculine and serious; leather-notebook direction (dark leather cover, cream paper, black ink, brass details, red ribbon). Interactive like a physical bullet journal (reference he liked: an interactive 3D planner with cover, page turns, tabs, stickers).

## Evidence on Hand

Only the owner's own data. No testimonials, no other users, nothing to fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Honest, not punishing: falls are logged with reflection and the counter restarts; victories are counted too.
2. Daily first: the today page must load fast and be completable in under two minutes.
3. Faith is woven in, not bolted on: verses and prayer sit next to the habits they support.
4. Private by default: nothing renders without the password.
5. His notebook: personalizable, tactile, worth opening.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Must work one-handed on a phone and with keyboard on laptop; respect reduced-motion.
