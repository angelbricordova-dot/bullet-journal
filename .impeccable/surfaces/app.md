## Scope
Whole app shell + all sections (Hoy, Retos, Progreso, Fe, Dinero, Lienzo, Ajustes) + login cover. Mode: Operate.

## Audience / job
Alejandro, daily on phone and laptop: complete the day's check-in in under two minutes, keep streaks, reach the "momento difícil" escape in one tap, see a year of progress.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A leather-bound field journal a man keeps for his own reform, not a habit-tracker dashboard. Refuses the card grid of stat tiles and progress rings.
OWN-WORLD: Oxblood/saddle/black leather with blind-tooled border and brass corners; red silk ribbon; ivory laid paper with ruled, dotted or grid pages; iron-gall ink (blue-black), vermilion for ledger rules and stamps; engraved small caps (IM Fell) over a sturdy grotesque (Archivo) for writing; rubber-stamp and wax-seal stickers in crisp vector.
STORY: Opening the cover with his password is a ritual; every tab is a section of one book; falls are written down and the count restarts; victories are stamped.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: open book, spread fills the viewport; left page = today's rapid log, right page = habit ledger, sleep/wake, gratitude; leather index tabs on the right edge; ribbon marks today; brass "Momento difícil" clasp at the top. Mobile: single page, tabs as leather strip at the bottom.
FORM: pinned. seed: none (pinned) — "Cuaderno de cuero: portada de cuero oscuro, papel crema, tinta negra, detalles en latón y letras con serifa" and "interactiva como bullet journal" (open cover, page turns, tabs, stickers).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
